# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Ingenious AI open-source projects is open to everyone who
acts in good faith. We expect:

- Respectful, technical discussion — critique code and ideas, not people.
- Honest claims: no overstating what a tool or a result proves.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks in any project space.
- No spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or malicious code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that break
this standard. Report problems to **orders@getingeniousai.com**.
